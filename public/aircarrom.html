Official Carrom Rules & Controls

🎯 2-Step Positioning & Slingshot Aiming

1. Use the Striker Slider below the board (or drag horizontally along the baseline) to position your striker.
2. Pull back from the Striker on the board to set power and aim. Watch the Ghost Target Preview to see the exact point of contact and coin deflection angle! Drag back into the center of the striker to cancel a shot.

👑 The Red Queen & Cover Rule (3 Points)

Pocketing the Red Queen requires you to Cover it by pocketing one of your own color coins on the same shot or the immediate next shot. If you fail to pocket your own coin on the cover shot (or commit a foul), the Queen is returned to the center!

⚪⚫ Turns & Scoring

White shoots from the bottom baseline; Black shoots from the top baseline. Pocketing your own color coin keeps your turn going. Pocketing an opponent's coin awards that coin to the opponent.

⚠️ Striker Foul ("Due" Penalty)

If the Striker falls into a pocket, your turn ends immediately and one of your pocketed coins is returned to the center (or owed as a Due penalty if you have none yet).
