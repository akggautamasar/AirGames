Barricade Race by AirGames

Outsmart your rival. Reach the other side.

Player 1 (You)
Computer

Drag a barricade from the controls onto the board, or tap a barricade type and then tap the board. Preview your move, then Confirm or Undo. Tap a highlighted square to move your pawn. Each player has 10 walls. Walls cannot overlap, cross or completely block a player's route. First pawn to the opposite edge wins.
