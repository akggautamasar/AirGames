🧱 Barricade Race

Race to the opposite edge. Block your rival, but always leave a path.

How to play: teal starts at the bottom and aims for the top; pink starts at the top and aims for the bottom. Each turn, move one square or place a two-cell wall. Select a wall direction, then tap the upper-left cell of its two-cell span. Walls may not cross, overlap or block every path. When pawns meet, jumps are allowed; if the space behind is blocked, diagonal sidesteps are allowed. AirBot currently uses shortest-path movement.
