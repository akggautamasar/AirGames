How to play · Rules
  1. Each player starts with three tiles on opposite corners of a 7×7 grid.
  2. On your turn, tap any unclaimed tile touching your territory orthogonally.
  3. After claiming, enemy tiles fully surrounded on all four sides by your territory or the board edge switch to your colour.
  4. Lightning tiles ⚡ cause a one-time burst, claiming neighbouring unclaimed tiles. They never automatically steal enemy tiles.
  5. When neither player has a legal move, the player controlling more tiles wins. Equal scores mean a draw.
